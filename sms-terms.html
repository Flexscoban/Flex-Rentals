SMS Terms & Conditions

Last updated: July 20, 2026

Program description

By providing your phone number and opting in on our website or application, you consent to receive SMS text messages from Flex Rentals LLC related to:

  • Application updates
  • Approval notifications
  • Pickup scheduling
  • Payment reminders
  • Maintenance reminders
  • Rental updates
  • Customer support

Message frequency

Message frequency varies based on your application and rental activity.

Message and data rates

Message and data rates may apply, based on your mobile carrier and plan.

How to opt out

Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe from SMS messages from Flex Rentals. You will receive one final message confirming your opt-out. After opting out, you may need to contact us directly for account or rental support.

How to get help

Reply HELP to any message for assistance, or contact us directly at (404) 738-6601 or info@joinflexrentals.com.

Consent is not a condition of purchase

SMS consent is not a condition of applying for or receiving a rental from Flex Rentals. You can apply and rent from us without opting in to SMS messages — we'll simply contact you by phone or email instead.

Supported carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Message delivery is subject to effective transmission from your network operator.

Privacy

Your phone number and SMS consent are used only for Flex Rentals communications as described above. See our SMS Privacy Disclosure and Privacy Policy for more information.
