FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

You must be at least 21 years old, have a valid driver's license, and upload the required documents. Rideshare or delivery applicants must also provide a screenshot of an active platform account.

Weekly rentals start at $350 per week. Final pricing depends on the vehicle, rental option and availability. Pricing is transparent with no hidden fees.

We offer both deposit and no-deposit rental options. Choosing a security deposit may qualify you for a lower weekly rate, while no-deposit options provide added flexibility. The best option depends on the vehicle and current availability.

We review your information and contact you to schedule a convenient pickup time. At pickup, you meet a Flex Rentals representative, review and sign the rental agreement, complete any remaining paperwork and receive the vehicle.

Yes. Vehicles are intended for approved rideshare and delivery platforms, including Uber, Lyft, DoorDash, Instacart, Amazon Flex and others.

Availability varies based on demand. Applications are reviewed quickly. If a vehicle is not immediately available, qualified applicants are contacted when a suitable vehicle becomes available.

Flex Rentals coordinates scheduled maintenance so renters can stay focused on driving. Service is required approximately every 3,000 miles or every 28 days, whichever comes first.

To help keep vehicles reliable and available, Flex Rentals vehicles must remain within Georgia. This supports insurance compliance, routine maintenance scheduling, and prompt roadside assistance and support. Special travel requests must be discussed with the team before the rental.
