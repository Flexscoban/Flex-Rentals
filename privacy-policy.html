Who we are

Flex Rentals LLC ("Flex Rentals," "we," "us") provides weekly vehicle rentals for rideshare, delivery, and personal use in the Atlanta, Georgia area. This policy explains what information we collect through flexrentalsllc.com (the "Site") and our rental application, and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you submit the application form on this Site, we collect the information you provide, which may include:

  • Full name, phone number, and email address
  • City and driver's license state
  • The rideshare or delivery platform(s) you intend to drive for

We may also collect standard technical information automatically, such as browser type, device type, and general usage data, to help us maintain and improve the Site.

How we use your information

We use the information you submit to:

  • Evaluate and process your rental application
  • Contact you about your application, your rental, or account updates
  • Send confirmation, scheduling, and support communications
  • Maintain records required for our rental operations

We do not sell your personal information.

How we share information

We may share information with service providers who help us operate our business — for example, our customer relationship management (CRM) platform, payment processors, and insurance or verification partners — solely to support the services described above. We do not share your information with third parties for their own marketing purposes without your consent.

Data retention

We retain application and rental information for as long as needed to operate our business, comply with legal and insurance obligations, and resolve disputes, after which it is deleted or anonymized in line with our internal retention practices.
